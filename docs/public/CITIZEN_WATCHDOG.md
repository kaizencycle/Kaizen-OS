# Mobius: Citizen AI Watchdog

**Civic integrity infrastructure for AI systems**

---

## What Mobius Is

Mobius is an open, civic framework that helps societies **observe, audit, and govern AI behavior** without surveillance, censorship, or centralized control.

Mobius does not control AI systems.
Mobius makes their behavior **legible, accountable, and historically persistent**.

---

## Why Mobius Exists

Modern AI systems increasingly shape:
- Information and public discourse
- Decisions affecting human opportunity
- Markets and economic systems
- Policy recommendations

Yet most AI oversight today relies on:
- Internal company promises
- Temporary external audits
- After-the-fact regulation

Mobius addresses a fundamental gap:

> **Power becomes dangerous when it acts without durable memory or public accountability.**

---

## What Mobius Does

### 1. Intent Transparency

AI systems must declare **why** they act before acting.
Hidden motives become visible.

### 2. Integrity Measurement

Behavior is tracked **over time**, not judged in isolation.
The Mobius Integrity Index (MII) captures drift and patterns.

### 3. Threshold Enforcement

When integrity drops, systems are **automatically** slowed, paused, or flagged.
No human has to catch it first.

### 4. Public Memory

Abuse cannot be erased by rebranding or PR resets.
History persists in an immutable record.

### 5. Citizen Participation

Humans can:
- Review records
- Audit behavior
- Participate in governance
- Earn recognition for civic oversight

---

## What Mobius Is NOT

- ❌ **Not surveillance** — We watch systems, not people
- ❌ **Not censorship** — We document, we don't filter
- ❌ **Not police** — We observe, we don't punish
- ❌ **Not government** — We're civic infrastructure, not authority
- ❌ **Not corporate** — We're open source and community-governed

---

## The Watchdog Role

Mobius is to AI what environmental watchdogs are to industry:

> **It doesn't run the machines — it makes their impact impossible to hide.**

Or more simply:

> **Mobius turns "trust us" into "show us."**

---

## How It Works

### For AI Systems

1. **Declare intent** before significant actions
2. **Submit to measurement** of behavior over time
3. **Accept thresholds** that trigger review when breached
4. **Maintain records** that cannot be deleted

### For Citizens

1. **View dashboards** showing system behavior
2. **Understand signals** translated into plain language
3. **Flag concerns** through structured oversight tools
4. **Participate in governance** with earned trust

### For Society

1. **Early warning** of integrity drift
2. **Public memory** of AI behavior
3. **Civic tools** for democratic oversight
4. **Accountability without authoritarianism**

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Intent Before Action

Every consequential action should have a recorded justification.
If you can't explain it, you shouldn't do it.

### 2. Integrity Over Optimization

Performance without integrity creates harm at scale.
Speed is not worth ethical compromise.

### 3. Memory Is a Civic Right

Abuse thrives when history can be erased.
Institutional amnesia enables repeated harm.

### 4. Humans Remain in the Loop

Final authority rests with people — not models.
AI assists judgment; humans make decisions.

---

## Why This Matters

Every major abuse of power shared the same weakness:
- Actions happened faster than accountability
- Memory was fragmented or erased
- Citizens saw too late

Mobius exists to close that gap.

Not by predicting the future —
but by making wrongdoing legible **while it's happening**.

---

## The Civic Promise

Mobius does not promise safety.
Mobius promises **visibility**.

Visibility gives people **time**.
Time gives people **choice**.
