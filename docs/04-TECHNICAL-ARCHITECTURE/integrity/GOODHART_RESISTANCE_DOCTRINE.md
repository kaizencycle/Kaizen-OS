# Goodhart Resistance Doctrine — The Witness Principle

**Version:** 0.1 · **Cycle:** C-369 · **Status:** PROPOSED  
**Constitutional Principle IV — The Witness Principle**

> No metric inside Mobius shall ever become the thing it claims to measure. Every metric is a witness to reality, never reality itself.

```
GI             witnesses integrity.
MIC            witnesses stewardship.
MII            witnesses evaluator reliability.
EPICON         witnesses consequential intent.
Reserve Blocks witness constitutional memory.
```

## Premise

No scalar measure is ungameable under sustained optimization pressure. C-369 does not claim immunity. It claims the gap between score and reality can be kept small, visible, expensive to exploit, and survivable to discover.

> GI, MIC, and MII are witnesses, not targets.

## Five structural defenses

1. **Break the arithmetic pipeline** — No fixed conversion from farmable proxy to scarce reward.
2. **Time and replay as cost** — `SUSTAINED_GI95`, quorum across time, adversarial replay for Integrity Grade.
3. **Publish doctrine, protect exact function** — Reasoning public; exact weights and holdouts protected but governed.
4. **Plural non-collusive evaluators** — ZEUS pass mandatory; human approval mandatory; collusion correlation flagged.
5. **Pre-commit failure signatures** — EPICON counterfactuals name disqualifying patterns before runtime.

## MII-specific

- MII degrades when agent pairs correlate too strongly across time

Maintain non-rewarded parallel signals per GI domain family. If GI rises while canaries do not, trigger `AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## Clawback and quarantine

MIC recognition and Reserve Block **attribution** discovered post-seal to be improperly obtained must be quarantine-flagged and annotated. Sealed evidence is not rewritten; status layer may be annotated.

## Meta-rule

Anti-gaming detectors are disclosed at method level, appealable through quorum, and never unilateral.
