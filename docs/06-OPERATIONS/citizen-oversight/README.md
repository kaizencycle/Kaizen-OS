# Citizen Oversight Module

**"See power while it's being exercised."**

---

## Purpose

Citizen Oversight exists to answer one question:

> "Can ordinary people see, understand, and respond to AI power while it is being exercised?"

Not afterward. Not in hindsight. **In real time.**

---

## What Citizen Oversight IS

- A **visibility layer** for AI system behavior
- A **comprehension system** that translates signals into plain language
- A **participation framework** for civic engagement
- A **memory architecture** that prevents institutional amnesia

---

## What Citizen Oversight IS NOT

- ❌ Not surveillance of individuals
- ❌ Not censorship of content
- ❌ Not centralized control
- ❌ Not punishment infrastructure

Mobius watches **systems with power**, not people.

---

## Oversight Pillars

### 1. Visibility (See)

Citizens can:
- View integrity metrics (MII)
- See intent declarations
- Track system behavior over time
- Observe changes, not just snapshots

No raw surveillance. Only behavioral transparency.

### 2. Comprehension (Understand)

Every signal must be:
- Human-readable
- Plain-language summarized
- Contextualized historically

If citizens can't understand it, oversight has failed.

### 3. Participation (Act)

Citizens can:
- Flag integrity concerns
- Submit evidence
- Request review escalation
- Participate in governance rituals
- Earn MIC for civic oversight work

Participation is opt-in, accountable, and recorded.

### 4. Memory (Remember)

Oversight records:
- Cannot be deleted
- Cannot be quietly altered
- Cannot be "PR-washed" away

This prevents rebranding abuse and institutional amnesia.

## Citizen Roles

| Role | Access | Capabilities |
|------|--------|--------------|
| Observer | Read | View dashboards, subscribe to updates |
| Auditor | Read + Flag | Flag anomalies, attach evidence |
| Steward | Full | Governance participation, review escalations |

All actions are logged. No anonymous power.

---

## What Citizens Oversee

**CAN oversee:**
- Integrity drift
- Unexplained behavior changes
- Threshold violations
- Repeated harm patterns
- Accountability gaps

**CANNOT:**
- Spy on individuals
- Access private data
- Control AI outputs directly
- Censor speech

---

## Example Oversight Flow

```
1. MII drops below baseline
2. ZEUS flags anomaly
3. Public dashboard updates
4. Citizens see:
   - what changed
   - when
   - why it matters
5. Auditors submit evidence
6. Steward review triggered
7. Outcome logged permanently
```

No secrecy. No panic. No erasure.

Mobius does not promise safety.

---

## Design Philosophy

### Non-Negotiables

Before any dashboard design:

1. **No raw personal data** — Never exposed
2. **No model internals** — Black box preserved
3. **No outputs surveillance** — Content not monitored
4. **No individual tracking** — People are not watched

This dashboard shows **system behavior over time**, not people.

If a citizen can't explain what they're seeing in one sentence, the UI has failed.
