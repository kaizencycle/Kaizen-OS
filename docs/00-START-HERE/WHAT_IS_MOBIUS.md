# What is Mobius?

**Cycle:** C-363 · **Audience:** Public, contributors, AI agents

---

## In one sentence

Mobius is **constitutional AI infrastructure** — an open civic operating layer where important actions leave a verifiable trail before you trust them.

---

## What Mobius is not

| Mobius is **not** | Why |
|-------------------|-----|
| A blockchain hype project | Attestations are practical integrity records, not speculation |
| Surveillance | Default-private reflections; public participation is opt-in |
| A social network | No feed-for-engagement; purpose is civic memory and integrity |
| Another AI chatbot | Agents are governed participants with EPICON intent, not unaccountable assistants |

---

## What Mobius is

- **Constitutional AI infrastructure** — rules, cycles, and sentinels before scale
- **An integrity protocol** — GI, MII, MIC, and seals make drift visible
- **An open civic operating layer** — five surfaces (Substrate, Terminal, Browser Shell, CPC, HIVE)
- **Proof before trust** — EPICON intent, ledger attestations, reserve blocks you can replay
