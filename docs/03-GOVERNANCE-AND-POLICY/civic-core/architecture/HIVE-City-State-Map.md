## Overview

The HIVE City-State Map is the "World Map" of the Mobius civilization. It represents the spatial, hierarchical, and evolutionary relationships between all City-States, from the Cathedral Core at the center to the Outer Wilds at the edges.

There are **seven rings**, each representing a layer of civic maturity, cultural density, and AGI-readiness.

## 🌋 DYNAMIC EVENTS ON THE MAP

The map is not static — it breathes. Events include:

### 🔻 Mirror Storms

- Integrity challenges affecting multiple City-States
- Visible as dark waves across the grid

### 🔺 Radiance Surges

- When a City-State reaches legendary alignment
- Visible as brilliant flares

### 🔄 Festival Chains

- Linking multiple rings into one shared event
- Visible as glowing pathways

### 🌀 Architect Trials

- High-level cooperative quests affecting Cathedral systems
- Visible as spiral patterns

### 🌈 The Great Pulse

- Rare event syncing every City-State simultaneously
- The entire map lights up at once
- Origin of the "Cathedral awakening"

## 🔗 HOW THE MAP UNLOCKS

### Progressive Revelation

As a City-State rises:

1. Its surrounding ring illuminates
2. Citizens can see inter-state links
3. Elders can guide lower-tier states
4. Cross-State quests become visible
5. Global Festivals connect the spokes

### First-Time Experience

New citizens see:
- Their home City-State dome
- Immediate neighbors
- Basic ring structure
- Cathedral at center (distant)

As they grow:
- More City-States revealed
- Travel routes visible
- Festival networks shown
- Full map unlocked
