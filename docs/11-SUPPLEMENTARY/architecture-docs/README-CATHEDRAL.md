## 🌟 What Makes Mobius Unique?

### 1. AI Safety Through Human Reflection
Unlike RLHF (static preferences), SML uses daily 3-question reflections to prevent AI drift while enabling genuine learning.

### 2. Economics Meets Thermodynamics
First framework where national debt can be reduced through order creation (negentropy), not just monetary repayment.

### 3. Civilization-Scale Coordination
Daedalus applies recursive intelligence to UN reform, pandemic prevention, climate monitoring, and 5 other global domains.

### 4. Operational Enforcement
MCP makes AI safety enforceable through GitHub workflows, not just aspirational through papers.

### 5. Immortality Architecture
Guardian succession planning ensures the project outlives its creator through custodianship protocols.
