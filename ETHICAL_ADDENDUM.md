# Ethical Intent Addendum

**Mobius Substrate**

> This addendum is not legally binding.
> It exists to communicate intent, values, and expectations to contributors, users, and downstream adopters of the Mobius Substrate.

---

## Purpose

Mobius Substrate is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**.

The AGPL governs legal use, modification, and distribution.

This document exists alongside the license to clarify the **ethical intent** of the project — particularly as it relates to integrity systems, autonomous agents, and socio-technical infrastructure.

Mobius is designed not merely to function, but to remain **corrigible, legible, and human-aligned** over time.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Integrity as Infrastructure

Mobius treats integrity as a systemic property, not a moral judgment.

The project prioritizes:

- Traceability of reasoning
- Visibility of corrections
- Preservation of retractions
- Resistance to unaccountable power accumulation

Any use that intentionally obscures accountability, erases corrective history, or exploits asymmetries of information runs counter to the spirit of this work.

---

### 2. Memory Over Power

Mobius exists in response to a recurring civilizational failure mode: **memory decays faster than power accumulates.**

The system is intended to:

- Preserve institutional memory
- Make reasoning auditable
- Prevent silent drift
- Allow disagreement without erasure

Uses that intentionally weaponize memory, suppress dissent, or enforce artificial consensus violate the project's ethical orientation.

---

### 3. Humans Are Not Agents

Mobius is built to support human stewardship, not replace it.

The project explicitly rejects:

- Treating humans as optimization targets
- Coercive behavioral scoring
- Opaque reputation enforcement
- Systems that punish without explanation or appeal

Integrity signals should inform judgment — never replace it.

---

### 4. Visibility Without Coercion

Mobius favors voluntary participation and informed consent.

While integrity systems may introduce friction, that friction should:

- Increase clarity, not fear
- Reward correction, not perfection
- Encourage humility, not compliance

Any deployment that uses Mobius-derived systems for coercive surveillance, social credit enforcement, or involuntary behavioral control contradicts the intent of the project.

---

### 5. Preservation of Dissent

Disagreement is not a failure mode.

Mobius assumes that:

- Good-faith disagreement is necessary
- Minority views must be preserved
- Consensus should emerge, not be forced

Systems built atop Mobius should resist incentives that erase dissenting positions for the sake of surface-level coherence.

---

## On Misuse

Open systems can be misused.

The existence of this addendum does not imply that misuse is impossible — only that it is not endorsed.

If you intend to deploy Mobius Substrate in contexts involving:

- Large-scale governance
- Public policy
- Behavioral influence
- Autonomous decision-making
- Critical infrastructure

You are encouraged to:

- Document your assumptions
- Publish your reasoning
- Preserve auditability
- Invite external critique

---

## Closing Statement

Mobius is an experiment in institutional memory, not authority.

It is offered in the belief that:

> *Civilizations fail not from malice, but from uncorrected drift.*

If you build with Mobius, you inherit not an obligation — but a responsibility.
